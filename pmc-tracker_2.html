Your data syncs automatically to Google Sheets. Every workout you add is saved instantly. Use the Sync button in the top bar to pull the latest data from the sheet — useful when switching between devices.

About

Mirrors the TrainingPeaks PMC algorithm. TSS = (duration_hrs × IF² × 100). Data syncs to your personal Google Sheet — accessible from any device at any time.
