Algorithm constants

These match TrainingPeaks defaults. The exponential formula: CTL = CTL_prev + (TSS − CTL_prev) × (1/42)

Set seeds to 0 when importing full history. If you know your current numbers from TrainingPeaks, enter them here and log forward from today.

About this tool

Built to mirror the TrainingPeaks Performance Management Chart algorithm. TSS is calculated as (duration_hrs × IF² × 100). Import your historical data via TrainingPeaks CSV export, then use the Week Planner to project your CTL/ATL/TSB forward. All data is stored locally in your browser.
